# Inferock · Serverless inference, with credits back for failed calls.

**Open** · fit 9 · Wed Oct 14 · 2 Offers · `inferock.ai`

**Register:** Sign up with an email address at `inferock.ai/app/signup`, then choose Watch or Serve.⁵

**What it is:** **Watch** records each LLM call’s quality, latency and cost on your own OpenAI, Anthropic or Gemini keys, free. **Serve** is prepaid pay-per-token inference through Inferock, with a 10% fee on supplier cost.¹ ² ⁴

**For you:** Fits *model access: gateways and inference, especially with free credits*, and Watch is free.⁴ ⁶

**Offers**

- Signup credit for Serve, a one-time gift if recorded for your account · *no card · no code · amount not stated · within the monthly cap*¹ ⁴
- **Yours to act on:** Credits back on failed calls, spent on Serve · *enrolling in the credit program, with your provider keys connected · capped at $1,000 a month · expire after 12 months*² ⁴

**Note:** Serve's models and terms are confirmed during onboarding; the rates on the pricing page are only an example.³ ⁴
