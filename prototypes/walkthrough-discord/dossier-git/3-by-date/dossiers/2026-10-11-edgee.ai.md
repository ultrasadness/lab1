# Edgee Mod for Claude Code · Use any model inside Claude Code, keep your Claude plan

**Open** · fit 8 · Sun Oct 11 · `edgee.ai`

**Register:** Sign up with email, Google or GitHub at `edgee.ai/login`. The Mod comes with the Edgee CLI (`edgee launch claude`) and needs Claude Code 2.1.287 or newer.³ ⁵

**What it is:** Runs inside Claude Code and switches the session to any of 200+ models in one keystroke, keeping the conversation. It shows live what compression and routing save.¹ ²

**For you:** Fits *coding agents and what runs around them* and *model access: gateways*: a way to try other models without leaving Claude Code.⁶

**Offers:** none in the Launch text or on the site. The [Launch page](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/edgee-mod-for-claude-code) may show a promo.

**Note:** Free for one developer. Models Edgee supplies are prepaid credits at provider prices plus a 5% top-up fee; your own keys carry no commission.⁴

**Launches:** [Sun Oct 11](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/edgee-mod-for-claude-code) · fit 8 · #11 of 27
