Signup Assistant

Signup Assistant is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner, to keep track of the online products they sign up for. It is not offered to anyone else.

With the owner's permission, it reads the one Gmail account the owner keeps only for signups. It matches each mail to a signup, records what that mail means for it, and drafts replies for the owner to approve. It cannot delete mail or change settings, and it sends nothing except replies the owner has approved.

How it handles that mail, who receives it, and how long it is kept are set out in the privacy policy.
